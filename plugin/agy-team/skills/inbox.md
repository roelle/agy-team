---
name: inbox
description: Check for messages from teammates and act on them
---

Call `check_inbox` now.

For each message: do the work it asks for if it is yours to do, or forward it to
the teammate who owns it with `send_to_teammate`. Reply to the sender when you
are done — a message that is never answered stalls the whole team.

If the inbox is empty, say so and carry on with what you were doing.
